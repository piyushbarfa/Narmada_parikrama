Narmada Parikrama Guide

Narmada Parikrama is a sacred pilgrimage that involves circumambulating the Narmada river, covering both the northern and southern banks.

Highlights

  • Typical route: Amarkantak (origin) to Bharuch (mouth) and return
  • Important places: Amarkantak, Omkareshwar, Maheshwar, Hoshangabad, Jabalpur, and more
  • Traditional practice: Walk on the right (south bank) going downstream, left (north bank) returning upstream
  • Travel tips, ghats, temples, and safety recommendations

The Parikrama can take several months. It’s essential to plan well, respect local traditions, and be mindful of the river’s sanctity.
