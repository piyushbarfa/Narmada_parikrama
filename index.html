About the Holy Narmada

The Narmada, also known as Rewa, is the fifth longest river in India and one of the seven holy rivers of Hinduism. Flowing westwards over a length of around 1,312 km, it plays a magnificent role in the ecology and culture of Central India.

Originating from the Amarkantak Plateau, the Narmada traverses picturesque landscapes, renowned for its ancient ghats and timeless temples. Its journey to the Arabian Sea is marked by spiritual significance, an unmatched pilgrimage, and breathtaking natural beauty.
